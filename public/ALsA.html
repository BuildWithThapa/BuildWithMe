Eight years. So many memories, so many little moments, so many reasons to fall in love with you all over again. And still, when I think about my favourite person in this whole world, it's you. It has always been you.

You were born on Maha Nawami — the ninth night, when the goddess's power finally becomes victory, when the whole valley smells of incense and marigold and the house fills up with family you haven't seen all year. I used to think that was just a nice coincidence. Now I think it fits you perfectly: you walked into my life the same way that day always does — loud with joy, warm with people who love you, and somehow, right at the center of it all, quietly unforgettable.

Thank you for being part of my life, for the smiles that make hard days softer, for the memories I replay in my head, and for all the ordinary moments that become special just because I get to share them with you.

I can't promise every day will be perfect, but I promise I'll keep choosing you, keep learning you, keep making you laugh, and keep finding new ways to love you. If I could go back and do it all again, I'd still look for you in every lifetime.

Today is your day, but honestly, you make every day brighter just by being in it. So this Dashain, as everyone puts tika on your forehead and jamara in your hair, know that mine is already there, every single day, whether you can see it or not.

Eight years behind us, forever ahead of us. You're my favourite hello, my hardest goodbye, and my sweetest forever. I love you more than these words can ever fit. 😚

Always yours, in every lifetime. ♥
